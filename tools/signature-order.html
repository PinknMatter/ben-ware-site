Signature sequencer

Click along the signature in the order the pen should travel. Each click snaps to the traced centreline, and the stretch between clicks is filled in by following the ink — so it can never cut across white paper. The preview animates with the site's own timing.

How

  1. Click where the pen starts.
  2. Keep clicking along the stroke — every centimetre or so, and one on each corner.
  3. Press N wherever the pen lifts, then carry on.
  4. Copy the output over the reveal array in src/components/Signature.astro.
